# Contributors

This repository documents a collaborative bioinformatics benchmarking project developed within the research and computational environment of **Nainsense Labs Private Limited**.

The project evaluates a reproducible whole-genome sequencing (WGS) variant-calling workflow using GIAB HG001 chromosome 22 reference regions.

## Project Lead

### Ritika Rajendra Rawat

**Bioinformatics Research Assistant · Bioinformatics Lead**

Ritika led the design and development of the benchmarking study and was responsible for the overall computational and analytical direction of the project.

**Contributions:**

* Designed the overall benchmarking strategy and workflow.
* Developed and configured the WGS variant-calling workflow.
* Performed computational preprocessing and quality-control analysis.
* Implemented workflow components for alignment, variant calling, VCF processing, normalization, and filtering.
* Designed and performed GIAB-based benchmarking.
* Conducted performance evaluation and statistical analysis.
* Interpreted benchmarking results and workflow limitations.
* Generated analytical figures, tables, and performance summaries.
* Structured the repository for reproducibility.
* Prepared project documentation and scientific interpretation.
* Led the preparation of the associated research output.
