# Final Publication Angle

Date: 2026-08-07
Owner: Ritika

## Final Research Direction
Benchmark-aware WGS variant-calling workflow with structured metabolic-health interpretation-ready outputs.

## Full Framing
This study develops a reproducible WGS variant-calling workflow validated against GIAB HG001 chromosome 22 benchmark regions, formally evaluated using RTG vcfeval, and extended into a structured interpretation-ready framework for preventive metabolic-health genomics.

## Why This Is Stronger Than a Basic FASTQ-to-VCF Pipeline
A basic FASTQ-to-VCF workflow is already common. The strength of this project is that it combines:
1. GIAB benchmark validation
2. RTG vcfeval formal benchmarking
3. Error analysis of missed variants
4. FASTQ-to-VCF technical validation
5. Structured metabolic-health interpretation output
6. Nainfit preventive-health relevance

## Correct Claim
We are developing an analytically benchmarked and interpretation-ready preventive genomics workflow.

## Claim We Should Avoid
We are not claiming full clinical diagnosis, clinical utility, or replacement of a certified clinical genomics laboratory.
